# 30-day trust plan

**One goal:** after 30 days, an F0 who followed along wants to take whichever course
we recommend to them: BYFA-101 or any other.

**Who F0 is:** has heard "AI", "agent", "MCP", "automation" many times, has used a
chatbot, maybe copied a ready-made workflow once. Has never built one agent that does
their own real job from start to end. Not clueless: do not re-explain what they already
know; give them the depth they have not felt yet.

Post copy for the Vietnamese audience is quoted in Vietnamese; everything else is in
English, like the rest of `docs/`.

## Why people take a course on someone's word

A recommendation works when four things are true for the person hearing it. Each week
of the plan builds one of them, in this order.

| Week | What the follower must feel |
|---|---|
| 1 | **"You already helped me."** I got a real result on my own job from your free content. |
| 2 | **"Your judgment is honest."** You say what not to learn, show your own mistakes, and recommend things you earn nothing from. |
| 3 | **"I have felt the gap."** I hit a wall myself, and I can see what lies past it. |
| 4 | **"The advice is about me."** The course you point me to fits my job and my level, and you said who it is not for. |

## Rules for all 30 days

1. Every post gives the viewer something to do in 15 minutes or less, on their own job.
   Knowledge alone does not count.
2. No selling for 21 days: no course link, no "sắp mở khóa".
3. Show the failure next to the success. Admitting what still does not work is what
   makes the rest believable.
4. Recommend only what you have used yourself. For every paid option, also name a free path.
5. Talk about their work (reports, customer replies, weekly numbers), not about AI.
6. Answer comments and messages one by one. Each reply is a small consultation: you are
   helping, not convincing.
7. From day 1, keep one channel you own (a Zalo group or an email list) where the
   exercises and replies live. Social media is where people find you, not where you keep them.

## The spine: one agent, built in public

For the 30 days the instructor builds one agent for one real, boring job, for example
"summarise weekly sales from a spreadsheet and send it to Zalo", and posts the progress,
the numbers and the mistakes. It proves skill without claims, and gives the audience a
story to follow (episodes #1 to #4 below).

## Week 1 (D1-D7): "You already helped me", quick wins

By D7 every active follower has made AI do one piece of their own job.

| Day | Post (hook) | The viewer does |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | "Bạn đã xem đủ clip về agent rồi. Hôm nay làm đúng 1 việc thôi." | Lists 3 repetitive tasks from their week |
| D2 | Chatbot and agent side by side on the same task: the chatbot answers, the agent opens the file, computes and sends | Picks the one task that repeats, follows rules and has clear input |
| D3 | "Viết quy trình bằng tay trước khi đưa cho AI." | Writes the task as 5 to 7 numbered steps |
| D4 | The instruction: role, steps, output template | Turns their steps into an instruction and runs it once in a chatbot |
| D5 | Build in public #1: the job I picked and my steps | Runs the same instruction on 3 real inputs |
| D6 | "3 chỗ AI hay sai trong việc văn phòng": numbers, dates, names | Checks their 3 outputs for those errors |
| D7 | Zalo: a share-your-result thread, and the instructor answers every post | Posts their result and one question |

## Week 2 (D8-D14): "Your judgment is honest", taste and candour

Followers start to trust what you call good or bad.

| Day | Post (hook) | The viewer does |
|---|---|---|
| D8 | "Mới bắt đầu thì đừng học 5 thứ này." (for example multi-agent systems before one working agent, or a framework before a clear job) | Crosses out what they were about to learn |
| D9 | Build in public #2: where my agent went wrong this week, with the log | Nothing; they watch the real mess |
| D10 | Honest review of a popular free tool: what it is good for and where it breaks | Tries it on their task and compares |
| D11 | A calm, specific look at a "AI làm thay 100%" claim: what is true and what is missing (no names, no mockery) | Nothing; they learn to spot hype |
| D12 | Free resources I really use, made by other people, that earn me nothing | Bookmarks one and does its first exercise |
| D13 | Live, 30 minutes: fix 3 followers' agents on screen, including one I cannot fix yet | Sends in their case |
| D14 | "Mình đã sai điều gì khi mới bắt đầu với agent." | Replies with what they got wrong |

## Week 3 (D15-D21): "I have felt the gap", it runs but can I trust it?

Followers hit the wall themselves and see what is on the other side.

| Day | Post (hook) | The viewer does |
|---|---|---|
| D15 | "Chạy 5 lần, ra 5 kết quả." The same task run 5 times, with the differences shown | Runs their agent 5 times on one input and counts the differences |
| D16 | Why: the model is the only part that guesses; everything around it can be controlled | Nothing; just the idea |
| D17 | What the agent may touch: give it only the data it needs | Removes one input the agent does not need and runs it again |
| D18 | Check before you send: one test that catches a wrong number | Adds one check and runs it 5 times |
| D19 | Definitions and templates: the same question gets the same answer | Adds one definition and runs it 5 times |
| D20 | Build in public #3: my agent's real right and wrong count, before and after | Posts their own before and after count |
| D21 | "Từ đây trở đi, tự mò sẽ chậm ở đâu": an honest map of the next hard parts (real data, tools, testing, privacy) | Picks the wall they hit |

D16 to D19 use the four-part frame (model, environment, checks, configuration). Credit
its source, and use our own examples, not another course's analogies.

## Week 4 (D22-D30): "The advice is about me", the recommendation as a service

Each person gets a learning path that fits them. A course is one step on that path.

| Day | Post or action |
|---|---|
| D22 | Publish the learning map: 3 profiles (office worker, report and data person, shop owner) at 3 levels, each with a free next step and a paid next step |
| D23 | Zalo: "Gửi mình 3 dòng: bạn làm việc gì, đã làm tới đâu, đang vướng ở đâu. Mình gợi ý bước tiếp theo." |
| D24-D26 | Answer every request one by one with a specific next step: sometimes a free resource, sometimes a course (ours or someone else's), sometimes "chưa cần học, làm tiếp bài tuần 3". Post examples with names removed. |
| D27 | How I choose what to recommend: it is hands-on, it works on your own job, someone reviews your work, and you can get a refund. For each course, who it is NOT for. |
| D28 | Build in public #4, the last one: the agent after 30 days, the hours it saves, what still fails |
| D29 | Live Q&A on the map. Recommend BYFA-101 to the people it fits, and say who should not join. |
| D30 | Thank-you post: what the group built in 30 days (their results, not ours), and the recommendation once more |

## Day-30 test

The plan worked if, when you post "Ai muốn mình gợi ý khóa tiếp theo cho đúng việc của
bạn?", people reply with their situation instead of asking the price. They are asking
for your judgment, not for a deal.
